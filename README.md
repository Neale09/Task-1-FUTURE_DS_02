# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, product demand, outlet efficiency, and customer preferences. The dashboard helps business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators and identify opportunities to improve revenue and operational performance.

## Business Problem

Blinkit operates across multiple outlet locations and product categories. Understanding sales trends, outlet performance, and customer purchasing behavior is essential for making data-driven decisions that improve profitability and business growth.

## Objectives

* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Analyze sales trends across outlet establishments.
* Compare sales performance across outlet sizes and locations.
* Evaluate outlet type efficiency and customer preferences.
* Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Dashboard KPIs

* Total Sales: $1.20M
* Average Sales: $141
* Average Rating: 3.9
* Total Items Sold: 9K

## Key Insights

* Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales revenue compared to Tier 1 and Tier 2 outlets.
* Supermarket Type 1 contributed the largest share of total sales.
* Fruits, Snacks, and Household products were among the highest revenue-generating categories.
* Regular fat-content products generated more revenue than low-fat products.
* Sales remained relatively stable over the years with a significant peak in 2018.

## Business Recommendations

1. Expand operations and inventory availability in Tier 3 locations due to strong sales performance.
2. Increase marketing focus on high-performing categories such as Fruits, Snacks, and Household products.
3. Replicate the successful operational strategies of Supermarket Type 1 outlets across other outlet formats.
4. Optimize inventory for low-performing categories to reduce storage and operational costs.
5. Monitor customer ratings and improve service quality to enhance customer satisfaction and retention.

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Modeling
* Data Visualization

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive filters for Outlet Location, Outlet Size, and Item Type.
* Sales trend analysis over time.
* Product category performance analysis.
* Outlet location and outlet type comparison.
* Fat content and customer preference analysis.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Blinkit's business performance and enables management to make informed decisions regarding inventory planning, outlet expansion, marketing strategy, and revenue growth.
